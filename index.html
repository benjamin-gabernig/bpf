01Manifesto

The Basel Peace Forum brings people from diplomacy, business, civil society and research into one room for one day. Not to agree, but to listen across lines.

The identity is two brackets and the space between them. It holds any word, in any language, and never closes. That’s the job of a forum.

02Identity system

One mark.
Every conversation.

The brackets never change. What sits inside does: a theme, a session, a city, a name. Every touchpoint, from badge to stage, becomes a held space.
